# Rust to Shine

A 3D restoration game. Soak rusty tools and toys in vinegar, wire-brush the rust away, sand, sharpen, hammer out dents, swap broken parts, repaint and polish, then drag the before/after slider to see the difference.

Thirteen items rotate through the jobs, and several have their own trick: magnet up a toolbox's spilled bits, wind a pocket watch, stencil a garage sign, tune an old radio, and spin a skateboard's seized wheels free. Finished jobs earn coins for paint colors and power tools (pressure washer, drill brush, orbital sander and more). The settings menu covers sound, vibration, the missed-spot glow, tool lift and graphics quality. Leave mid-job and it picks up where you stopped.

Play it at https://brandrisk4.github.io/Rust-to-Shine/ and add it to your home screen:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app".

It works offline after the first load. If you change any file, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed copies update.
